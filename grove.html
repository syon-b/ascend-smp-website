GEM DOSSIER · FIVE TIERS + ASCENSION

🟢 Grove

Grove combines survival economy, disorienting floral traps, and heavy terrain control.

Each tier adds to the last unless an ability explicitly replaces an earlier one. Exact effects may be balanced during testing.

← Browse all gems

Tier abilities

01

T1 · Nature’s Fortune

Guaranteed 2× drops from naturally generated ores and fully grown crops. Excludes ancient debris, player-placed ores, and Silk Touch. Fortune still works.

02

T2 · Bewildering Garden

Create a 5-block-radius flower garden for 10s. Enemies entering suffer reversed movement inputs for 5s if feasible server-side, otherwise a strong movement-disorientation substitute. 60s cooldown.

03

T3 · Wild Blessing

Permanent Speed I and Luck II anywhere while Grove is equipped.

04

T4 · Vine Prison

Trap a targeted enemy in unbreakable vines for 5s. Ender pearls or teleport abilities can escape. 60s cooldown.

05

T5 · Golden Harvest

Harvested carrots automatically become golden carrots. Normal golden apples grant 2 additional absorption hearts.

ASC

Ascension · Earthshatter

lift a 3×3×2 chunk of earth and throw it up to 15 blocks. Direct impact deals 2 hearts of true damage and creates a temporary pile that traps enemies, who must mine or teleport out. 90s cooldown.

Playing this gem

Start by learning your first two abilities, then build toward the advanced combo at Tier 4 and the signature Tier 5 unlock. Ascension requires Tier 5, 10 energy, and the Ascension ritual.

Check recipes for crafting ingredients and progression for upgrade requirements.
