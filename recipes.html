Recipes

Each recipe has a proper 3×3 table diagram, a separate result slot, and an ingredient list. Detailed tooltips are available by hovering over items.

About these layouts

Some planned recipes cost more than nine individual items. Those recipes are illustrated using stacked items, which requires custom Paper crafting validation rather than an ordinary vanilla shaped recipe. These are design layouts, not proof they have already been implemented.
