GEM DOSSIER · FIVE TIERS + ASCENSION

🌈 Prism

Prism is a chaotic elemental gem combining lightning procs, freezing, explosive mobility, and golem support.

Each tier adds to the last unless an ability explicitly replaces an earlier one. Exact effects may be balanced during testing.

← Browse all gems

Tier abilities

01

T1 · Thunderflame

Permanent Fire Resistance. Sword attacks have a chance to strike the opponent with custom lightning.

02

T2 · Frostbite

After a 3-hit combo, the opponent gains freezing for 10s.

03

T3 · Fireball

Shoot a ghast-like fireball. It can launch you without self-damage or hit an opponent for fire damage.

04

T4 · Elemental Momentum

Placing blocks while moving causes an ice-like skid that propels you forward. Two consecutive axe crits set the opponent on special fire for 5s; water cannot extinguish it, but Fire Resistance still works.

05

T5 · Elemental Guardians

Summon 4 enhanced snow golems that help you and occasionally shoot fireballs.

ASC

Ascension · Energy Ritual

target one player or hostile mob for 15s. They burn with unextinguishable fire, continuously accumulate freezing, cannot jump, and every 10s have a 10% chance to be struck by custom lightning for 3 hearts. Each successful melee critical hit multiplies ritual fire damage by 1.2×, capped at 2×. 180s cooldown.

Playing this gem

Start by learning your first two abilities, then build toward the advanced combo at Tier 4 and the signature Tier 5 unlock. Ascension requires Tier 5, 10 energy, and the Ascension ritual.

Check recipes for crafting ingredients and progression for upgrade requirements.
