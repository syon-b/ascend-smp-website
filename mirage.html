GEM DOSSIER · FIVE TIERS + ASCENSION

🟣 Mirage

Mirage begins as a stealth gem and becomes an illusion gem. NPC clones appear only at higher tiers.

Each tier adds to the last unless an ability explicitly replaces an earlier one. Exact effects may be balanced during testing.

Tier abilities

01

T1 · Low Profile + Silent Steps

Hostile mobs have 40% reduced detection range. Your footsteps are silent, and ordinary walking, sprinting, and sneaking do not trigger sculk sensors or shriekers.

02

T2 · Vanish

Gain Invisibility and Speed I for 10s. 60s cooldown. Attacking ends invisibility.

03

T3 · Shadow Step

Short-range mobility blink/teleport. Existing behavior kept.

04

T4 · Mirror Clone

Summon one realistic NPC clone with your appearance. Existing behavior kept.

05

T5 · Shadow Assassin + Mirror Master

Keep two NPC clones and clone swapping. Additionally, opening PvP from invisibility with a valid backstab deals 1.5× damage, refreshes Shadow Step, and reduces Vanish and Mirror Clone cooldowns by 50%. 90s internal cooldown.

ASC

Ascension · Phantom Master

upgraded clone deception with brief invisibility and multiple swaps. 120s cooldown.

Playing this gem

Start by learning your first two abilities, then build toward the advanced combo at Tier 4 and the signature Tier 5 unlock. Ascension requires Tier 5, 10 energy, and the Ascension ritual.

Check recipes for crafting ingredients and progression for upgrade requirements.
