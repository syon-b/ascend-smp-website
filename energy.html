Energy

Energy & Combat Resonance

Energy ranges from 0 to 10. Players start at 5. Kills give +1 energy and PvP deaths cost 1 energy.

Energy effects

EnergyEffect
0Gem disabled
1–2Scalable gem effects are 20% weaker
3–4Scalable gem effects are 10% weaker
5–7Normal effectiveness
8–95% bonus to eligible numerical gem effects
1010% bonus to eligible numerical gem effects

Combat Resonance

During PvP, effective energy can temporarily shift by up to ±2 based on performance. This does not permanently change stored energy.

  • Good combat sequences can increase effective energy.
  • Taking burst damage or getting disrupted can reduce it.
  • Resonance resets after combat.
