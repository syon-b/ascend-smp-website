Commands

All player and admin commands for the gem system. Use /gem for everything as a player.

Player commands — /gem

CommandWhat it does
/gemOpen your gem ability overview GUI
/gem switchOpen the gem switching menu and pay to change your active gem
/gem upgradeOpen the upgrade menu to spend resources and advance your gem tier
/gem ascendPerform the Ascension ritual — requires T5, 10/10 energy, 1 Ascension Stone, 4 Netherite Ingots, and 3 Gold Ingots
/gem infoShow details about your currently active gem
/gem info <gem>Show details about any specific gem (e.g. /gem info rupture)
/gem abilitiesList all your unlocked abilities and their current cooldown status
/gem recipesOpen the crafting recipes GUI
/gem energyDisplay your current energy level

Using abilities

  • Right-click while holding your gem crystal to activate your selected ability
  • Shift + Right-click to cycle through your unlocked active abilities
  • Your selected ability and cooldown are always shown in the action bar when holding the crystal
  • Prism players: press F (swap offhand) to toggle shot mode

Admin commands — /gemadmin

Ops only. Not visible to regular players.

CommandWhat it does
/gemadmin set <player> <gem> <tier>Set a player's gem and tier in one command. Tier is 1–5 for T1–T5, or 6 for Ascension
/gemadmin gem set <player> <gem>Force-switch a player's active gem (no cost, no cooldown)
/gemadmin gem tier <player> <tier>Set a player's gem tier directly
/gemadmin gem ascend <player>Grant Ascension to a player's active gem
/gemadmin gem reset <player>Reset a player's gem progress entirely
/gemadmin energy set <player> <amount>Set a player's energy to a specific value
/gemadmin energy add <player> <amount>Add energy to a player
/gemadmin energy remove <player> <amount>Remove energy from a player
/gemadmin energy info <player>View a player's current energy and gem info
/gemadmin cooldown clear <player>Clear all ability cooldowns for a player
/gemadmin cooldown clear <player> <ability>Clear one specific ability cooldown
/gemadmin switch reset <player>Reset a player's switch count and cooldown
/gemadmin switch bypass <player>Bypass the switch cooldown for a player
/gemadmin info <player>View full gem and energy info for a player
/gemadmin reset <player>Full reset of a player's gem data
/gemadmin reloadReload the plugin config
