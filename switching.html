Gem switching

Gem Switching Costs

Players can choose a different gem directly. Switching gets progressively more expensive. Previously unlocked tiers and ascensions remain saved per gem.

Paid switchCostCooldown
1st3 Crying Obsidian + 1 Amethyst Block + 4 Diamond Blocks + 16 Amethyst Shards10 minutes
2nd6 Diamond Blocks + 3 Crying Obsidian + 2 Amethyst Blocks + 24 Amethyst Shards20 minutes
3rd10 Diamond Blocks + 4 Echo Shards + 3 Netherite Scraps40 minutes
4th12 Diamond Blocks + 6 Echo Shards + 2 Netherite Ingots60 minutes
5th+16 Diamond Blocks + 8 Echo Shards + 1 Netherite Block90 minutes

One free trial switch is planned within the first 24 hours. It should not increase the paid switch counter. Switching is disabled during combat and for 60 seconds afterward.
