Progression

Tiering & Ascension

Each gem has five upgrade tiers. Reaching Tier 5 and 10 energy unlocks eligibility for Ascension.

Tier upgrades

UpgradeResourcesExtra requirement
T1 → T28 Amethyst Shards + 4 DiamondsNone
T2 → T316 Amethyst Shards + 8 Diamonds + 1 Echo ShardNone
T3 → T42 Echo Shards + 4 Diamond Blocks + 2 Blaze RodsQualifying PvP encounters or PvE trials
T4 → T54 Echo Shards + 4 Diamond Blocks + 1 Nether StarBoss trial

Ascension requirements

  • Reach Tier 5 with the gem
  • Reach 10/10 energy
  • Craft an Ascension Stone
  • Perform the final ascension ritual

Ascension creates a special upgraded version of the gem and unlocks its final signature power.
